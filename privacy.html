Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Purpose

Personal Research Backup Utility is a personal-use utility used by the owner to back up, synchronize, and restore research files between cloud compute instances and the owner's Google Drive account using rclone.

Google User Data

The utility accesses Google Drive data only as necessary to upload, download, synchronize, verify, and restore files belonging to the owner.

Data Storage

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's rclone configuration and remain under the owner's control.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for purposes unrelated to the owner's personal research backup workflow.

Data Retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive account until the owner deletes them. OAuth access can be revoked by the owner through their Google Account security settings.

Limited Use

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the personal backup, synchronization, and restore functionality described above.
